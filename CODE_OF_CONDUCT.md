# Code of Conduct

I want `fast-attnres` to be a place where people can compare ideas and
evidence without having to tolerate personal attacks. Everyone who participates
in the project is expected to help keep discussions technical, welcoming, and
safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while checking claims carefully.
- Discuss code, methods, evidence, and their effects rather than a person's
  identity, ability, or motives.
- Welcome questions from people at different levels of experience.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Credit prior work, reproducible artifacts, and other contributors.
- Protect private information, credentials, and unpublished results.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention, unwanted contact, or sharing sexualized
  material.
- Doxxing or publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberate disruption, impersonation, trolling, or bad-faith reporting.
- Presenting another person's work as your own, falsifying results, or altering
  an evaluator to manufacture a correctness or performance result.
- Any conduct that would be unsafe or unlawful in the project context.

## Enforcement

Please report unacceptable behavior privately through GitHub's reporting tools
or through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive details in
a public issue. I will review reports, protect the privacy of the reporter as
far as practical, and decide on an appropriate response. Depending on the
severity, responses may include a request to stop, a correction, moderation of
content, temporary participation limits, or removal from the project.

Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in an
investigation is not acceptable.
